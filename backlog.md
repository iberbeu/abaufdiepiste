### UI-12 — New generation app (plan)

**Order of the remaining tasks** (decided 2026-09-26 — functionality first, polish last): UI-12.12 → UI-12.13 → UI-12.14 → UI-12.11 → UI-12.15 → UI-12.17. UI-12.16 (tests) runs alongside every task.

Decided 2026-09-26: stays plain HTML/CSS/JS (no Flutter). Built in parallel as a new app in `app/v2/`; the old app keeps working until the switch-over (UI-12.17). No save compatibility with v1 (no users yet). `game_logic.js` and its tests are reused unchanged — this is a rebuild of the UI layer only.

Guiding principles: one main step fills the screen and the app moves on by itself once it is done; optional actions (Sehenswürdigkeit, Gratis Fahrt, Joker, …) are small, recognisable icon buttons at the top or bottom — never hidden in the menu; a compact score strip sits at the top; everything else (scores in detail, settings, reference) lives behind a menu. Playful and game-like look. Portrait first, but landscape must work. No handover screen between turns. No sound or haptics for now. FEAT-24 (virtual Punkteblock) is part of this refactor.
